Make these in Fish Audio with "The Umbra" voice, download each one, and save it
here as line1.mp3 ... line24.mp3. Then run:   python tools/import_voice.py
(or tools/build.py, which runs it too). Missing lines just show as subtitles.
Delivery: understated. She is tired and certain, never shouting except line 7.

The old takes for the first story are in old_story/ (not used any more).
